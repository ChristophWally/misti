# Section 8.4: Modal Verb "dovere" (to have to/must/to owe) - Complete Forms

## Overview
The modal verb "dovere" is the most architecturally complex verb in Italian, serving dual functions:
1. **Modal usage**: "to have to/must" (expressing necessity/obligation)
2. **Transitive usage**: "to owe" (expressing debt/obligation to someone)

This dual nature creates unique compound form patterns and auxiliary inheritance rules that are critical for database architecture.

## Entity Meta Values Structure
All forms use consistent metaattr021val123 (modal-verb) classification throughout the conjugation system.

## Auxiliary Inheritance Rules

### Modal + Infinitive Patterns
- **Transitive complement**: dovere + avere auxiliary

  - "sono dovuto/a andare" (I had to go)
- **Reflexive complement**: dovere + essere auxiliary + agreement
  - "mi sono dovuto/a alzare" (I had to get up)

### Agreement Patterns
- **With essere auxiliary**: participle agrees with subject
  - Marco è dovuto andare / Maria è dovuta andare
  - I ragazzi sono dovuti partire / Le ragazze sono dovute partire
- **With avere auxiliary**: no agreement (unless direct object precedes)
  - Marco ha dovuto studiare / Maria ha dovuto studiare
